About The Project

Our mission is to bridge the communication gap between the deaf and hard-of-hearing community and the rest of the world. By utilizing cutting-edge computer vision and deep learning, we've developed a system that makes interpretation accessible to everyone with a browser.

The core of the system is built using a robust tech stack including Python for logic, OpenCV for image processing, and MediaPipe for high-fidelity hand tracking.
